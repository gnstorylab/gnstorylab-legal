Privacy Policy for Kids & Families

Last Updated: September 5, 2026 • Published by GN Story Lab (Spain / European Union) • Version 1.1.5

👶 Summary for Parents and Guardians:
At GN Story Lab, children's privacy is our highest priority. Magic Puppet Theatre DOES NOT collect any personal data from children. We do not require accounts, real names, or emails from kids. All voice recordings and puppet movies are stored 100% locally on your own device and are never transmitted to external servers.

1. Scope and Legal Compliance

This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled within the mobile application Magic Puppet Theatre (the "Application"), developed and published by GN Story Lab (based in Spain / European Union) for distribution on authorized platforms including Google Play Store. This policy strictly complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the United States, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in the European Union (Article 8 concerning children), and the Google Play Families Policy.

2. Information We DO NOT Collect

The Application is intentionally designed from the ground up to be a completely private, safe, sandbox environment:

  • No Account Registration for Minors: No user registration is needed to enjoy the game. GN Story Lab never asks for a child's name, age, physical address, or phone number.
  • No Location Tracking: We do not request or access precise or approximate GPS location data.
  • No Access to Contacts or Personal Photos: The Application never requests access to your phone book, personal photos, or device camera.

3. Microphone Permission & Voice Recording (100% Local Storage)

The Application includes a creative feature allowing children and families to record their voice during a puppet show and save their movies in "My Movies":

  • The RECORD_AUDIO microphone permission is only used actively when the user presses the Record button on stage.
  • Strictly Local Device Storage: Voice audio and saved movies are stored securely inside the Application's local sandboxed memory on your own device.
  • Zero Cloud Uploads: Audio recordings are NEVER transmitted over the internet, never shared with third parties, never analyzed with cloud AI, and never made publicly accessible.
  • Parents can delete any saved movie at any time from the "My Movies" screen.

4. Family-Friendly Advertising

If rewarded advertisements are enabled to unlock optional props:

  • We only use ad networks certified under the Google Play Designed for Families program (Google AdMob with the mandatory tagForChildDirectedTreatment: true setting).
  • No Behavioral or Personalized Tracking: No tracking cookies, advertising IDs, or cross-app profiling is performed on children.
  • All served advertisements are strictly filtered for family appropriateness (G-rated / PEGI 3).
  • VIP Pass purchasers enjoy a 100% permanently ad-free experience.

5. In-App Purchase (Lifetime VIP Pass - One-Time Payment)

The Application offers an optional one-time in-app purchase called "VIP Pass" (one-time payment of €3.99 for lifetime access) which unlocks additional cast members, unlimited actors, and permanently removes all advertisements:

  • No Subscriptions or Recurring Fees: This is a one-time, lifetime purchase with no automatic renewals or recurring charges.
  • Secure Official Billing: All financial transactions are processed directly and securely via Google Play Billing.
  • Taxes and Invoicing: Prices include applicable Value Added Tax (VAT) / sales taxes corresponding to the user's country of residence, collected and remitted through Google Play as official billing agent.
  • Right of Withdrawal & Refunds: In accordance with European Union consumer protection regulations and digital goods legislation, as digital content is provided immediately upon purchase, the user expressly acknowledges that performance begins at the time of purchase. Any refund requests are governed exclusively under official Google Play refund policies.
  • Purchase Restoration: Full support is provided to restore prior purchases at no additional cost on any Android device associated with the user's Google Play account via the "Restore Purchases" button.
  • Financial Privacy: GN Story Lab never receives, stores, or accesses credit card numbers or financial account details.

6. Parental Rights & Data Deletion

Because GN Story Lab does not store personal data on any remote server, parents have immediate and complete control over all data on their device. You can erase all local data by deleting individual movies or uninstalling the app.
